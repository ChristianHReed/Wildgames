# Credits

- **Shaped art (0.42 and 0.44):** survivors, aliens, nests, furniture, street props, cars, garden and building kit in `assets/shaped/` are made in code for Afterstreet (generators in `assets/shaped/src/`). The third-party models listed below are what is left; the list of what to replace is in `docs/ART_REQUESTS.md` section 12.

- **Maps Builder libraries:** flatbush, flatqueue and tinyqueue by Vladimir Agafonkin (ISC), polylabel and earcut by Mapbox (ISC), fflate and fzstd by Arjun Barrett (MIT), hyparquet by Hyperparam (MIT). Full licence texts in `builder/lib/LICENSES.md`.
- **Map data:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License. The built-in Springfield region comes from the Afterstreet map pack (`design/afterstreet_handoff/map_data/springfield_50mi/`), pulled from the Overpass API and OpenFreeMap vector tiles in October 2026, with elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM/3DEP); `tools/build_region.py` cuts it into squares.
- **Furniture and street props:** KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0 and KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0 by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), CC0. Converted and recoloured by `tools/build_models.py`.
- **Icons and interface design:** the Afterstreet design pack in `design/afterstreet_handoff/`.
- **Ground and indoor textures:** original, painted in code by `tools/paint_ground.py`; the ground cover and tree planting rules are in `docs/TERRAIN.md`.
- **Elevation:** Terrain Tiles hosted on AWS Open Data (Terrarium format, assembled by Mapzen from sources including USGS 3DEP and SRTM), downloaded by the game when a map is made.
- **Building fronts and roofs:** original, made in code by `tools/paint_buildings.py` and `play.html`; see `docs/BUILDINGS.md` for the references.
- **Survivor skeleton and moves:** the survivors' bodies, hair, hats, faces and clothes are original (`assets/shaped/`). They move on the skeleton of "Universal Base Characters" (free standard version) with clips from "Universal Animation Library" and "Universal Animation Library 2" (free standard versions), all by Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0; the packs themselves are not copied here.
- **Sound:** footsteps, impacts, glass, doors, cloth and metal are synthesised by `tools/synth_sounds.py`. Gunfire, alien calls, rain, wind and thunder are made by the game.
- **Toolkit, tire, car battery and engine parts icons** are drawn by `tools/build_vehicles.py`, and the clothing and seed packet icons by `tools/draw_clothes.py`.
- **Trees, plants, furniture, the truck and sounds:** since 0.95.0 all original: trees and plants (`assets/shaped/flora.js`), the thirteen furniture pieces (`furniture2.js`) and the truck's door and tyre (`props.js`) are shaped models by the content chat (`tools/build_nature.py` bakes the near models and the far-tree pictures); the sound effects are the content chat's set (`assets/audio/sounds_content.js`) plus a few made in code by `tools/synth_sounds.py`. The older Kenney (kenney.nl, CC0) Nature Kit, Furniture Kit, Car Kit, Impact Sounds and RPG Audio are no longer used.
