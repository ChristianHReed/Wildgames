A survival journal on real streets

Afterstreet

First-person survival in your own city, built from OpenStreetMap. Everything runs from this folder: no install, no internet.

Survivor
Settings
Make a map
Needs internet once; after that the map works offline. Type a town, neighborhood or address.
Big region
A region is far larger than a map: up to 50 miles in every direction. It is cut into squares about 3.4 miles across. Each square downloads the first time you travel into it and is then kept on this device, so it needs internet only for squares you have not visited or downloaded.
Saves
Workshop: make items and manage packs
Items you design here can be crafted in the game from wood and scrap. Stronger items cost more.
Play together
To host, start your world and press Invite player. To join a friend's world, paste their invite code here. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, hotspot or cable.
Send this reply code back to the host. The game starts when they press Connect.
  1. Go to openstreetmap.org and zoom in on a neighborhood (about 1 km across works best).
  2. Click Export at the top, then the blue Export button. It downloads map.osm.
  3. Put it in the maps folder next to this file, or pick it with Load map file.

Up to 6 players. No server or internet is used; devices connect directly. Version 0.16.
