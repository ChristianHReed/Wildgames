Maps from the Maps Builder
Load a map file (.asmap) you made or were given, then press Play on it. You can also drop map files or edit files (.asedit) anywhere on this page. Maps stay on this device and work offline.
A region is far larger than a map: up to 50 miles in every direction. It is cut into squares about 3.4 miles across. Each square downloads the first time you travel into it and is then kept on this device, so it needs internet only for squares you have not visited or downloaded.

Play together
To host, start your world and press Invite player. To join a friend's world, paste their invite code here. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, hotspot or cable.
