The crew
Four playable survivors for local co-op. Chunky, friendly proportions: the head is about a third of total height, limbs are rounded tubes, and every shape gets the same 4 px dark-brown outline as the world assets. Each player owns one accent color, which also tints their map dot and HUD bar.
