# Content chat reply: car interiors and the glass rule (1 Nov 2026)

**Done (cars, in `workshop/designs/cars.js`, in `packs/workshop-crafts.js`):** hatchback, sedan, wagon, suv, offroader, pickup, crew_pickup, flatbed, van, rebuilt with a hollow cabin: carpeted floor, headliner, door cards, dashboard with an instrument binnacle, a steering wheel on the left (pieces named `steering wheel`), centre console and gear lever, seats with backrests and headrests. Four-seaters have 4 seats (driver first, then passenger and two rear), pickups, flatbed and van 2, the crew pickup 4. Windows are thin glass panes (windscreen, rear, quarter glass); each door is a lower panel plus its own glass pane and frame, named together so the whole door swings. Same length, width and height as before.

**Points for the game** (asset `points`): `cabin.eye` (kind eye, the driver's eye; e.g. sedan x -0.4, y 1.26, z -0.03; dir forward) and `steer.wheel` (kind hinge: pivot at the wheel centre, dir = the column). The Workshop has no steering-wheel rig job yet, so turn the pieces named `steering wheel` about `steer.wheel` in the game, or ask the Workshop chat for a job. The game also needs to know the driver is on the LEFT (x < 0).

**Glass rule (from the user):** glass is a thin tinted pane everywhere. All remade vehicles, boats, aircraft and the military set now use panes of 4 cm or less, the stock display cases and counters (14 glass blocks in interiors, services, props, farm) were rebuilt as five thin panes, and new pack glass tints can be named `glass...` (loader and Workshop export now keep them). Not changed: the old stock cars' single poly cab-glass volume (the remade cars replace them), round glass (barber pole, bird cage, projector, teller dome), lanterns, church windows (8 cm), `machines` truck windscreen decals.

**Not yet:** buses, fire engine, motorhome and trucks have a driver seat but no dashboard/wheel/eye point (their walk-in rooms are the interior); military 4x4s have no dash; wrecks of the remade cars use the damage generator.
