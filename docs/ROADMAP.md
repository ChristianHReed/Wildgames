# Afterstreet development roadmap

Last updated: 26 October 2026, at version 0.104.3 (rebuilt from the content chat's and map chat's deliveries). This one document holds both the game features still to build and the free art and sound packs each step needs.

The design (what the game is and why) is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md); this page is the build plan. How to use it: work down the "Next versions" list. When a version needs a pack, the "Assets" section says where to get it and which download to pick. Download the zip and attach it to the chat; Claude converts it into the game's `assets` folder.

Features were compared with Project Zomboid, DayZ, 7 Days to Die, The Long Dark, State of Decay 2, Valheim and Unturned, and for stealth with Alien: Isolation, The Last of Us and Thief. Those comparisons come from general knowledge of the games.

When one pack member hears or sees something the whole pack turns to it. At dawn a horde walks back to its nest and is gone. Pacing, from Left 4 Dead's director idea: no new horde pack while eight or more aliens are already on you, and the ordinary night trickle waits while a horde is still to come. Design notes taken from the genre (Project Zomboid, 7 Days to Die, Left 4 Dead, State of Decay): detection by movement, light and noise rather than by knowing the player's position; noise and mass fights draw a crowd; creatures lose interest and disperse rather than vanish; relief between peaks keeps pressure fair. |

## Risks to keep in mind

- Pathfinding and sight checks cost processing time; on the iPhone the number of aliens thinking each moment may need a cap.
- A two-storey home takes about twice as long to lay out as before. A few very large buildings already took one to three seconds in the test browser, which may show as a pause on the iPhone when you walk up to one.
- In multiplayer the host decides what aliens know. Guests do not yet see the awareness pill.
- Breakable buildings (Art 5 and 6): broken bays change pathfinding and sight, and in multiplayer the host has to share the damage with guests. Framed bays are far cheaper than the block plan, so whole streets can keep their detail.
- The new look's night uses a few real lights; on phones keep it to about six lamps near you plus alien eyes.
- Animation files are large. The 64 GB drive has room, but phones load the "lite" set, so animations may need a trimmed phone version.
