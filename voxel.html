Afterstreet voxel lab

One grid of 0.5 m blocks and one material table. Change a number and try it straight away: dig time, walking speed, how far a built block can reach without ground under it, whether it burns. The test world has a river in a channel (still water that flows toward the south), a gorge, a hill, a brick house with glass, a wooden bridge and a wooden tower. Dig with the right tool; light the tower; turn on rain and watch the bare ground turn to mud; dig under the bridge.
