LAST BLOCK

A survival game played on real streets. Load a map exported from OpenStreetMap and its buildings, roads, parks and water become your world. Search buildings for supplies, keep fed and watered, and outlast the nights.

  1. Go to openstreetmap.org and zoom in on a neighborhood (about 1 km across works best).
  2. Click Export at the top, then the blue Export button. It downloads a file called map.osm.
  3. Click Load map file here and pick it, or drag the file onto this page.

Also accepts Overpass JSON and GeoJSON. Shops, pharmacies, police stations and restaurants on the map hold different loot.
