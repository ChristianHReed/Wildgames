OpenStreetMap needed

Streets, buildings, names, addresses, shops, water, power lines, rail, fences and trees.

  • Big areas: your state from Geofabrik (US states) or Geofabrik (world). Pick the .osm.pbf file.
  • Small areas: openstreetmap.org Export (.osm), or the Download button here.

The file picker will not let you choose it? Drag the file from the Files app onto this page (iPad: use Split View), or onto any card.

Elevation recommended

Real ground: hills, valleys, creek banks.

  • Best (US): USGS National Map Downloader: choose Elevation Products (3DEP), then 1 meter DEM (or 1/3 arc-second DEM where 1 m is missing), draw your area, download the GeoTIFF files.
  • No files: tick the box and the builder fetches free terrain tiles (about 15 m detail) while it builds.

Overture buildings fills gaps

Buildings OpenStreetMap is missing, from Microsoft, Google and Esri footprints. Only added where OpenStreetMap has no building.

  • Overture Maps Explorer: turn on Buildings, move to your area, download (GeoParquet or GeoJSON).
  • Or the Overture download tool for a box (.parquet, smaller and faster to read).

Lidar later version

Laser scans: real building heights and roof shapes, and every tree. Reading lidar comes in a later version of the builder.

  • USGS Lidar Explorer (.laz tiles).

Aerial photos later version

Laid under the map to trace and check things by eye. Comes in a later version.

  • USGS National Map Downloader: Imagery, NAIP (.tif).

Just the changes you made in 3D (terrain, roads, buildings, props, floods), a small file to share. Anyone with the same area can add it on top of their own map.

Puts the map in the game's own list of regions. Works when the builder and the game are opened from the same place (the same website or the same drive) in the same browser.
